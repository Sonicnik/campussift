## Purpose and selection rules

Find events that help Sonic (1) meet students in his year or above, founders, builders, researchers, and professionals; (2) explore quant finance, investing, venture, startups, big tech, physics, mathematics, and computer science; or (3) simply make friends and enjoy Duke. An event can serve either goal or both. Prefer on-campus opportunities, especially small, interactive, recurring gatherings. Search off campus separately and show the cost of getting there.

Search by **host as well as topic**. A dinner hosted by the Cube, a game night hosted by a quant group, or a social organized by a physics lab can be relevant without having a career keyword in the title. Include open events even when they are hosted by a selective club; verify who may attend. For large employers (for example, Jane Street, JPMorgan Chase, OpenAI, or Apple), only report a specific event after verifying a current organizer listing and eligibility.

For each candidate, eventually record: organizer and original URL; date and time in Eastern Time; campus/location; likely attendees; conversation opportunities; recurrence; eligibility and membership requirements; registration deadline; admission and transport cost; door-to-door time; and confidence in the listing. Applications and deadlines may need a two-to-eight-week lookahead, whereas casual social events may appear at short notice. Deduplicate events that appear in several calendars.
